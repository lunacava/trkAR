Getting started

How to drive a Blender camera with the camera move you shoot with trkAR on an iPhone or iPad, and record and export takes. The app's screen and buttons are explained in the App guide.

The Blender add-on's labels are in English (0.11 and later). To use it in Japanese, set Interface Language in Edit > Preferences > Add-ons > trkAR Camera to 日本語 (Follow Blender, the default, shows Japanese when Blender itself is in Japanese).

1. What you need

  • An iPhone / iPad with ARKit (iOS 16 or later). Depth comes from the back camera of models with LiDAR (Pro models)
  • A Mac or Windows PC with Blender 4.2 or later (5.1+ recommended; sending the Blender screen over NDI needs 5.3+)
  • NDI Tools (free), for sending and receiving video
  • The iPhone and the PC on the same network. Wired Ethernet is the most stable; busy Wi-Fi makes video stutter

2. The app (iPhone / iPad)

  1. Install trkAR Cam (trkAR in the Japanese store) from the App Store and allow camera, local network and microphone
  2. Launch it in landscape and look around slowly. When WLDtrk at the top left shows Mapped, you are ready
  3. Set the floor. It is set automatically once the floor is found. If not, aim the center crosshair at the floor and tap AIM AT THE FLOOR ▸ TAP TO SET GND (or SET GND in the top bar)
  4. In ✱ (Settings), enter your PC's IP in PC IP Address and 8000 in Port (on a Mac: System Settings > Network; on Windows: ipconfig)

3. The Blender add-on

  1. Download trkar_camera-<version>.zip from Releases and drag and drop it onto the Blender window (or Edit > Preferences > Add-ons > ▼ > Install from Disk)
  2. On macOS, turn on Blender in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network, then restart Blender (the most common stumbling block)
  3. Try it with the sample scenes (subway platform, classroom)

4. Connect

  1. In the 3D view, press N to open the sidebar and open the trkAR tab
  2. Place the Blender camera where you want to shoot and press ▶ on trkAR Receive. Switch to the camera view (numpad 0) and move the iPhone: the camera moves the same way
  3. To use the live video, turn on CAM on the iPhone and choose <iPhone name> (trkAR) as the live video (NDI) source in trkAR Receive. To keep only the person, also turn on the iPhone's MASK (or use RVM, see "Cutting out people" below). If nothing shows the live video yet, a screen is placed in front of the camera when receiving starts (it stays transparent until video arrives, so it never hides the CG)
  4. To shoot while watching the CG on the iPhone, send the camera view with NDI Output and choose <PC name> (Blender (trkAR)) with MONI on the iPhone

Cutting out people (two ways)

To put the person from the live video into the CG, you need a cut-out of the person (alpha). There are two ways to get it, and both work the same afterwards.

To set up the RVM relay, get this repository (git clone https://github.com/lunacava/trkAR.git, or Code > Download ZIP on GitHub) and follow the tools/matte README. Then set RVM Relay Folder in Blender's Edit > Preferences > Add-ons > trkAR Camera to your local tools/matte. With Start Matte on Receive on, it starts together with ▶ on trkAR Receive.

5. Record and export

  1. REC in Recording & Takes, or REC on the iPhone, records the camera move, live video and depth while the timeline plays. Press it again or stop playback to finish
  2. Play Take plays a take back so you can fix the CG and lights. Back to Live returns to receiving
  3. Export writes the camera view to render.mp4
  4. To hand it to a video generation AI, Export Generation Inputs makes the intermediate files → Files for video generation

Takes are kept as take_<date>_<time>/ in trkar_takes/ next to the saved .blend.

Troubleshooting

ProblemCheck
The camera does not moveIf the panel's packet counter (Received n / UDP m) stays at 0: Blender's local network permission, the PC IP Address and Port on the iPhone, and the same network. If Pose ○ Hz shows a number, poses are arriving
The CG floor and the real floor do not matchIs the floor set on the iPhone (the panel says iPhone floor not set otherwise)? Aim the crosshair at the floor and SET GND again
No NDI sourcesIs NDI Tools installed? Same network? Office and school Wi-Fi often blocks traffic between devices
Video stutters or lagsUse wired Ethernet for the iPhone / iPad and the PC. Set CAM Resolution and MONI Resolution to Low on the iPhone. Cool the iPhone if it shows HOT
The live video is gray or whiteThe 3D view is in Solid mode. Switch to Material Preview
