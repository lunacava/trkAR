App guide

trkAR measures the iPhone / iPad camera position and orientation with ARKit and sends it to your PC in real time. It can send the camera pose (OSC), the camera video (NDI), and depth / person masks (UDP / NDI), and it can show NDI video from the PC (such as the Blender CG) over the camera view. Without a PC, you can try AR with the demo scenes (SCN).

Download on the App Store (trkAR Cam)

Landscape only: hold the iPhone sideways. For the whole flow of shooting with Blender, see Getting started.

② Numbers at the top right

tx ty tz is the position (m) and rx ry rz the rotation (degrees) being sent to the PC. Once the floor is set, ty is the height above the floor. OSC 60Hz below them is the rate the pose is actually being sent (close to 60 on devices that run at 60 fps; a yellow 0 means nothing is being sent).

✱Settings (see below)

④ Center and ⑤ bottom left

  • The crosshair in the center is where the floor is set, distance is measured and focus is taken. While sending to the PC without a floor set, AIM AT THE FLOOR ▸ TAP TO SET GND appears below it. Aim the crosshair at the floor and tap it to set the floor
  • CTL: the virtual camera controller (see below). A small dot at its top right means an offset is still applied
  • POS: a controller that moves the demo creature (only while a demo is shown with SCN)
  • Close a controller with the × at the top right of the center area

First use

  1. Allow camera, local network and microphone (without local network, nothing reaches the PC)
  2. Look around slowly and wait for WLDtrk to show Mapped
  3. Set the floor (if it is not set automatically, aim the crosshair at the floor and tap SET GND)
  4. In ✱, enter PC IP Address and Port (8000 by default). OSC is sent from launch

After moving to another place, or when the numbers jump, tap Rst twice to start over.

Connecting to a PC

  • Camera pose: /client/current is sent over OSC every frame. Receive it with trkAR Camera in Blender, or an OSC In CHOP (matching port, Active on) in TouchDesigner. The format is in section 4 of INTEGRATION.md
  • Camera video (CAM): the NDI source <device name> (trkAR). Quality is CAM Resolution in Settings (Low = 720p / High = about 1080p). Use Low if the device gets hot
  • Depth and person mask (DPT): sent to UDP port 9100 at PC IP Address. On devices with depth (LiDAR on the back, TrueDepth on the front), also sent as the NDI source (trkAR-Depth). DPT Data in Settings chooses Mask only (person mask only, the default) or Depth (depth + person mask)
  • Watching the PC's video (MONI): choose a source (for example Blender (trkAR)) from NDI Source ▾ at the top left. If it is not listed, tap rescan. While video arrives you see a green LIVE and the address it is connected to, and below it the reception stats (resolution, fps, dropped frames, longest stall)

Locking focus (AF)

With autofocus, focus shifts back and forth whenever the subject changes, and the field of view breathes a little each time (so the CG field of view on the PC wobbles too). Aim the crosshair at the subject you want and tap AF in the top bar to focus there and lock. The number in AF-L 2.4m is the distance when locked. Tap again to return to auto. Focus Position in Settings moves the locked focus by hand (0 = near to 1 = far; it has no unit).

Controller (CTL)

Adds what you move with the sticks to the real iPhone motion before sending (supported by the Blender add-on).

Troubleshooting

ProblemCheck
Nothing reaches the PCSame network? Is Settings > trkAR > Local Network on for the iPhone? Are PC IP Address and Port right? If the OSC Hz at the top right is 0, tracking is lost (look around)
No NDI sourcesCheck the network and tap rescan. Office and school Wi-Fi often blocks traffic between devices
Video stutters or lagsIn the stats under MONI, check that the address after LIVE is on the wired side (it may connect over Wi-Fi). Cool the device if it shows HOT. Set CAM / MONI Resolution to Low. Turn off CAM / DPT / MONI you do not use
Numbers jump or driftLook around until Mapped. Dark places, plain white walls, and lots of mirrors or glass are hard. If it does not recover, tap Rst twice
The floor height is wrongAim the crosshair firmly at the floor and SET GND again
Focus shifts back and forthLock focus with AF in the top bar
