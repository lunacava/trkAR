trkAR turns your iPhone or iPad into a camera tracker for virtual production. It streams the camera position and orientation from ARKit to your PC (TouchDesigner, Blender and more) over OSC in real time.

  • Camera pose over OSC
  • NDI® video output, and NDI input from your PC overlaid on the camera view
  • LTC timecode input, and start / stop recording on your PC with a countdown
  • Depth (LiDAR / TrueDepth) and person-mask output
  • Ground-plane calibration (automatic once the floor is found) and focus lock
  • Demo scenes (AR animals) you can try without a PC

Download on the App Store (trkAR Cam)

Blender add-on

The Blender add-on trkAR Camera makes a Blender camera follow trkAR, brings in NDI video, and records and exports takes. It is on Releases. See Getting started for how to use it, Files for video generation for making inputs for video generation AI, and Integration for connecting it to your own cloud or tools.
